CLIENT AGREEMENT

Terms & conditions / service agreement

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Between: Duranex Software Solutions (“the Provider”)

and the Client (as identified in the onboarding process and subscription activation).

1. The service and scope

1.1. The Provider offers subscription-based website design, hosting, setup and maintenance, and custom software development.

1.2. The deliverables (plan, number of pages, features) are those of the plan chosen by the Client, or those set out in a written quote for custom work, and are agreed before the subscription is activated.

1.3. Website plans (Foundation, Growth and Pro) are informational websites. A page is a separate URL; a one-page site with several sections counts as one page. Custom software (such as web apps, dashboards, booking systems, automations, e-commerce, databases or user logins) is provided under the Custom and Software plan, by written quote.

1.4. Software projects are billed monthly. A “larger build” is software that (a) has user logins or a database, or (b) connects to third-party systems (such as WhatsApp API, Sage, Xero, SimplePay, payment gateways or OCR/AI services), or (c) would take more than about two weeks to build. Larger builds carry a minimum term of six (6) months. Other software jobs are month to month. The applicable term is stated in the quote.

2. Payment terms and billing

2.1. Advance payment: Services are billed on a flat monthly subscription basis, payable in advance. There is no setup fee.

2.2. Project commencement: No design work begins, and no onboarding questionnaire is processed, until the first monthly payment has cleared.

2.3. Turnaround: The Provider commits to delivering a website preview link within 7 business days of receiving the completed onboarding questionnaire and all required Client content. The period only starts once the Provider has received these, and delays in supplying them delay delivery accordingly. Timelines for software projects are set out in the quote.

2.4. Price lock: The Client's monthly price is locked for as long as the Client remains on the same plan. Moving to a different plan changes the price to that plan's price.

2.5. Recurring billing: Subscriptions are processed automatically each month through the Provider's payment gateway (Paystack).

2.6. Suspension: If a monthly payment fails or is not received by the renewal date, the Provider may suspend the Client's website or software until the balance is settled.

3. Client onboarding and content

3.1. The Client provides business details, logos, images and videos through the onboarding questionnaire.

3.2. Copywriting: Where the Client does not supply text, the Provider drafts website copy at no extra charge using AI-assisted tools. The Client reviews and approves it during the preview. The Provider may edit or condense text supplied by the Client to suit the web.

3.3. The Client confirms that all material supplied does not infringe any third party's copyright or trademark, and is responsible for the accuracy of facts stated on their site.

4. Intellectual property and ownership

4.1. Code and design: The website structure, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, design templates, layouts and server configuration are the intellectual property of the Provider. The Client receives a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the website while the subscription is active, unless the code is bought out under clause 6.4.

4.2. Client content: The Client keeps ownership of their own text, branding, logos and media.

4.3. Domain names: Domain names are registered in the Client's name, and the Client owns their domain.

5. Unlimited light maintenance

5.1. The monthly subscription includes unlimited light maintenance: minor text updates, adding or swapping images, changing contact details and adjusting opening hours.

5.2. Redesigns, new pages beyond the plan limit, new functional components and complete re-branding are outside light maintenance and need a plan upgrade or a quote.

6. Cancellation, upgrades and deactivation

6.1. Cancel anytime: The Client may cancel at any time, subject to any minimum term that applies to a larger software build (clause 1.4). To avoid the next billing cycle, cancellation must be requested in writing by email to hello@duranexsolutions.com at least fourteen (14) days before the next renewal date.

6.2. Deactivation: On the effective date of cancellation, the website or software is taken offline and the Provider's access arrangements are ended, unless the code has been bought out under clause 6.4.

6.3. Transfer: Except under clause 6.4, the Client may not copy, move or migrate the website's layout, files or source code to another provider.

6.4. Code buyout: The Client may buy the website's source code for a once-off fee equal to twelve (12) times the Client's current monthly plan price, provided the account is fully paid up. On payment, the Provider hands over the website files, and the Client may host the website anywhere.

6.5. Domain: Cancelling does not affect the Client's ownership of their domain name.

7. Limitation of liability

7.1. The Provider uses reliable systems and takes regular backups, but is not liable for loss of revenue, business interruption, third-party server downtime or data loss suffered by the Client.
