  1. 04:30

    Ovens on

    Suurdeeg BakehouseSourdough, slow, in Philippolis

    The deck oven takes an hour to reach 250 °C. While it heats, we turn out the loaves that proved overnight in the cold room and score them by hand. This page is our day, hour by hour.

    The clock above shows what’s coming out of the oven right now.

  2. 05:30

    First loaves out

    Country sourdough & wholewheat

    The bread we’re named for: flour, water, salt and a starter we’ve fed every day since the first loaf. Twenty hours from mixing to the oven. It needs an hour on the rack before we cut it, because the crumb is still setting.

    • Country white, 800 gR85
    • Wholewheat, 800 gR90
    • Half loaf, eitherR45
  3. 06:00 · first light over the koppies
  4. 06:30

    The dark breads

    Rye & caraway, and the baguettes

    One hundred percent rye goes in while the oven is at its hottest. It’s dense, sour and keeps for a week. The baguettes follow it: short, crackly, and best eaten in the car park.

    • Rye & caraway, 1 kgR105
    • BaguetteR40
  5. 07:00

    Doors open

    Coffee’s on, door’s unlocked

    The first loaves have cooled, the machine is warm, and there’s usually a queue of farmers and early cyclists. Flat white R38, rooibos R25. Full coffee list →

  6. 07:30

    Butter, folded in

    Croissants & pain au chocolat

    Three days of work: the dough on Wednesday, the butter folded in on Thursday, shaped Friday night. One tray each morning. When it’s gone, it’s gone.

    • Butter croissantR32
    • Pain au chocolatR38
  7. 09:00

    The sweet tray

    Cinnamon buns & cardamom knots

    Made with the croissant offcuts and a brioche dough, glazed while they’re hot. This is the tray people set alarms for. Arrive at 09:00 and you’ll smell it from the street.

    • Cinnamon bunR38
    • Cardamom knotR40
  8. 10:30

    Mid-morning

    Seeded loaf & melktert

    Sunflower, linseed and pumpkin seeds soaked overnight, so the loaf stays moist for days. Next to it, a melktert in a sourdough-butter crust, cut once it’s cool enough to hold its shape.

    • Seeded loaf, 900 gR95
    • Melktert, a sliceR35
    • Melktert, whole (order a day ahead)R260
  9. 12:00

    Last bake of the day

    Olive & rosemary flatbreads

    The oven is cooling, which is just right for flatbreads: a quick, blistered bake with olive oil, rosemary from the yard and coarse salt. Good for lunch, better with a braai.

    • Olive & rosemary flatbreadR65
  10. 13:00

    Already thinking about tomorrow

    Starter fed, dough mixed

    While the shop sells the last loaves, the bench is floured again. Tomorrow’s dough is mixed, folded four times and shaped, then goes into the cold room for sixteen hours. That slow, cold rest is where the flavour comes from.
