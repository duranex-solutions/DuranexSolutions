04 Callouts

How a callout works

  1. 1Your alarm or panic button triggers. The signal comes straight to our control room in Centurion.
  2. 2A controller phones you and asks for your password. Wrong password, or no answer, and the car goes anyway.
  3. 3The nearest patrol car is sent. Our officers check the perimeter, gates and windows from outside.
  4. 4You get a short report on WhatsApp: what we found, what we did, and anything that needs fixing.

If police are needed, we call them and stay until they arrive. We never enter your house without you or a key-holder.
