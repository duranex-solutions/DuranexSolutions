Services & fees

Fixed fees, agreed before we start.

Every fee below is a starting point for a typical file. After the first meeting you get one number in writing, and it stays that number until your situation changes.

01

Bookkeeping

We capture every transaction from your bank feed, match it to an invoice or slip, and reconcile the accounts by the 10th of the next month. You get a one-page summary and a short list of anything we couldn’t place.

1 A transaction is one line on your bank statement. Most sole proprietors we see have 40 to 80 a month.

04

What the fee doesn’t cover

  • SARS’s own penalties and interest. We try hard to make sure there aren’t any, and we tell you straight away if one arrives.
  • Audits in the statutory sense. If your company needs an audit rather than a review, we’ll refer you to a registered auditor.
  • Estate work and trusts. We’ll point you to someone who does these every day.

Payment is by debit order on the 1st. You can leave with one calendar month’s notice, and we hand your file to whoever takes over.
