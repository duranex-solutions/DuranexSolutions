03

Questions people ask first

SARS auto-assessed me. Do I still need you?

Maybe not. Auto-assessments often miss out-of-pocket medical costs or a retirement annuity. Send it to us and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s worth changing.

I’m three years behind. Is that a problem?

It’s common and fixable. We file the oldest year first, ask SARS to waive penalties where there’s a reason, and set up a payment plan if tax is owed.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. With your permission we ask them for last year’s figures and handle the eFiling switch. No awkward phone call for you.

Do I have to come in?

No. Documents come by email or a shared folder, and meetings happen on a video call if you prefer.
