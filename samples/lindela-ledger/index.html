05

Questions people ask first

SARS auto-assessed me. Do I still need you?
Maybe not. If the auto-assessment includes everything, accepting it is fine. It often misses things like medical costs your scheme didn’t cover, or a retirement annuity. Send it to us and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s worth changing.
I’m three years behind. Is that a problem?
It’s common, and it’s fixable. We file the oldest year first, ask SARS to waive penalties where there’s a reason to, and set up a payment plan if there’s tax owing.
Can you take over from my current accountant?
Yes. With your permission we ask them for last year’s figures and working papers, and we handle the eFiling switch. You don’t need an awkward phone call.
Do I have to come in?
No. About half our clients have never seen the office. Documents come by email or a shared folder, and meetings happen on a video call.
