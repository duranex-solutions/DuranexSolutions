Geysers Repair & replacement

Cold shower, water through the ceiling, or a geyser breaker that keeps tripping? Most geyser faults are one of six parts. Here’s what they do and what each fix costs.

Geyser prices Not sure? Check in 3 taps
01

Inside your geyser

  1. 1
    ElementHeats the water. When it burns out you get a cold geyser or a tripping breaker.
  2. 2
    ThermostatSwitches the element on and off. A faulty one means lukewarm or boiling water.
  3. 3
    Pressure control valveKeeps mains pressure down to what the geyser is rated for.
  4. 4
    Vacuum breakersStop the tank collapsing when water drains out. Often the source of a drip on the roof.
  5. 5
    Drip trayCatches water if anything leaks and sends it outside, not through your ceiling.
  6. 6
    Safety valve and overflowLets off pressure and heat if the thermostat fails. A constant drip outside is a warning.

03

Signs it’s on its way out

  • Rusty or brown hot water
  • A damp patch on the ceiling under the geyser
  • The overflow outside drips all day
  • Banging or hissing when it heats
  • The geyser breaker trips more than once
  • It’s over ten years old and has never been serviced
04

Geyser burst? Do this first

  1. Geyser breaker off

    At the DB board. It’s usually labelled “geyser”.

  2. Cold supply closed

    The valve on the cold pipe into the geyser, or the main stopcock at the meter.

  3. Open a hot tap

    It takes the pressure off the tank.

  4. Photos, then call

    Your insurer will want them. Call 000 000 0000 (demo number), any time.
