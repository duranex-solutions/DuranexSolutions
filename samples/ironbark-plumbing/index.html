How a job runs

  1. You call or send the message

    Tell us what’s wrong and where you are. Photos help.

  2. We give you a two-hour slot

    And a WhatsApp when we’re on the way.

  3. Price agreed before work starts

    We find the fault, explain it, and quote. You say yes or no.

  4. Fixed, tested, cleaned up

    We test under pressure and leave the floor how we found it.

  5. Invoice the same day

    By email or WhatsApp, with photos. Card, EFT or SnapScan.
