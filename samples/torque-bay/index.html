What finished looks like

Beading is the easy part

Any fresh sealant beads water. What matters is the clean, corrected paint under it, and knowing how old your protection is. Every hand-over comes with a written sheet that tells you.

04

A Stage 2 day, sector by sector

S1S2S3S4
  1. S130 minWalk-round under lights. We note every chip, dent and scratch with you there, and send you the photos on WhatsApp before we start.
  2. S22 hWash and decontamination. Snow foam, hand wash, iron fallout remover, tar remover and clay. The paint has to be properly clean before anything touches it.
  3. S32.5 hInterior, wheels and engine bay. Done while the paint dries indoors, so no water spots.
  4. S41 hProtection and hand-over. Sealant on, a last check under the lights, then the spec sheet and keys back to you.
