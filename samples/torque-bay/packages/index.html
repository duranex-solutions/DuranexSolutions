04

Small print

Stone chips
Polishing can’t fill chips or scratches that catch a fingernail. We’ll show you which ones before we start, and you won’t pay for promises.
Ceramic
The car stays indoors overnight to cure. Don’t wash it for a week afterwards. Rain is fine.
Very dirty cars
Sand, pet hair or a farm-road bakkie takes longer. We quote the extra at the walk-round, before any work is done.
Deposit
Only for Stage 3 and 4: R1 000 to hold the bay, taken off the final bill.
Payment
Card or EFT when you collect.
