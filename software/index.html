Monthly pricing, not a big upfront bill

Smaller builds are billed month to month. Larger builds are billed monthly with a 6-month minimum. A build counts as larger if it:

  • has user logins or a database (client portals, job-card systems, staff rosters), or
  • connects to third-party systems (WhatsApp API, Sage, Xero, SimplePay, payment gateways, OCR or AI services), or
  • would take more than about two weeks to build.

Booking widgets, simple dashboards, calculators and form automations usually stay month to month. You'll always see the term in your written quote before you commit.

YOUR DATA

Built with POPIA in mind

Some software handles personal information, such as staff records, ID photos or certificates. For those projects, I'll ask what personal information is involved, collect only what's needed and help you decide who should have access.

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), your business remains responsible for the personal information it collects, and I'm not a legal adviser. I'll build sensibly, and you can confirm your obligations with a professional.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kind of software can you build?

Web apps, dashboards, booking systems, client portals and automations for small and medium businesses. If it's something your team does repeatedly by hand, it's worth a conversation.

How is software priced?

Monthly, based on a written quote after we've talked through what you need. There's no large upfront cost.

Is there a minimum term?

Smaller builds are month to month. Larger builds (user logins or a database, third-party integrations, or more than about two weeks of work) have a 6-month minimum. Your quote will state which applies.

What about my data and POPIA?

I'll ask what personal information the software will handle, collect only what's necessary and help you decide who gets access. Your business remains the responsible party under POPIA.
