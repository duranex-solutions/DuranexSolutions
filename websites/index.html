FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I own my website? What if I want to leave?

Your domain and your content (text, logo, photos) are yours. The website code stays with Duranex while you're subscribed, a bit like renting: the build, hosting and updates are covered by your monthly price.

You can cancel any time by emailing written notice at least 14 days before your next renewal. If you cancel, the site goes offline, unless you buy the code, which costs 12 times your monthly price, once-off. You receive the website files and can host them wherever you like.

Will my price go up?

No. Your monthly price is locked for as long as you stay on your plan. If you move to a bigger plan, you pay that plan's price.

How long does it take?

You'll have a private preview within 7 business days of me receiving your completed questionnaire and content. Smaller sites are often quicker. The faster you send your content, the faster you go live.

What if I don't have content?

I'll draft the copy for you, free of charge. It's AI-assisted, and you approve it in the preview before launch. If you send me your own text, I may edit or condense it so it works well on the web.

What about my domain?

Domains are registered in your name. If you already have one, I connect it. If you don't, I help you register one.

What does "unlimited light maintenance" cover?

Minor text changes, swapping or adding photos, updating contact details and changing opening hours. Send me a message and it's done.

New pages, redesigns and new features are outside light maintenance. They need a plan upgrade or a quote.

What counts as a page?

A page is a separate URL, such as Home, Services, About or Contact. A single-page site with several sections counts as one page.
